\# Band Saw

The \*\*band saw\*\* is often used to cut stock down to a usable size and can cut thick stock smoothly. It can also be used to cut curved lines, but it does not offer the highest level of precision.

\## Operating Procedure

1\. \*\*Complete all safety checks before using the machine.\*\*
   
   Make sure:
   
   \- Safety glasses are on.

   \- Long hair is tied up.

   \- Sleeves are rolled up.

   \- The area around the machine is clear.

2\. \*\*Place the part on the table.\*\*
   
   If the part is large, have one person support the end of the stock while the other operates the machine. This provides better control over the part.

3\. \*\*Adjust the blade guard height.\*\*
   
   Loosen the guard knob and lower the guard until it is approximately \*\*⅛–¼ in. above the top of the part\*\*. Tighten the guard knob fully.
   
   Keeping the guard close to the part reduces blade movement and minimizes the amount of exposed blade.

4\. \*\*Set up a guide if necessary.\*\*
   
   Use a right-angle or straight-line guide when making a straight cut. Use an appropriate push tool or other shop-approved method when needed to keep your hands safely away from the blade.

5\. \*\*Check the part before starting.\*\*
   
   Make sure the part is \*\*not touching the blade\*\* before turning on the machine.

6\. \*\*Turn on the band saw.\*\*
   
   Press the \*\*ON\*\* button on the left side of the machine.

7\. \*\*Make the cut.\*\*
   
   Maintain control of the part and slowly feed it into the blade. Keep the workpiece stable throughout the cut.

    !!! warning "Do not pull the workpiece backward"

        \*\*Never pull the part backward through the blade while the machine is running.\*\*

        If the part needs to be repositioned, stop the machine and wait for the blade to stop completely before readjusting it.

8\. \*\*Turn off the machine.\*\*
   
   Once the cut is complete, press the appropriate \*\*OFF\*\* control and wait for the blade to come to a complete stop.

    !!! danger "Emergency"

        If something goes wrong, use the \*\*emergency stop\*\* and follow your shop's emergency procedures.

9\. \*\*Clean up.\*\*
   
   After the blade has completely stopped:

   \- Remove the finished part and scrap material.

   \- Turn off the lamp.

   \- Clean the machine and surrounding work area.

\## Quick Safety Checklist

Before operating the band saw, verify:

\- [ ] Safety glasses are on.

\- [ ] Long hair is secured.

\- [ ] Loose clothing and sleeves are secured.

\- [ ] Work area is clear.

\- [ ] Blade guard is properly adjusted.

\- [ ] Workpiece is clear of the blade before startup.

\- [ ] You know the location of the emergency stop.
